Maintenance Plans

Maintenance plans that keep your vehicle clean

Once your vehicle is in good shape, we come back on a set schedule so dirt and grime never get a chance to build up. Plans are open to approved customers in La Vergne and the towns we serve.

Book Online Call (615) 339-9763

What a maintenance detail includes

Every maintenance visit is our Full Detail, inside and out, done at your home or office with our own water and power.

Inside

  • Interior vacuum
  • Compressed air blowout of cracks, crevices, and vents
  • Dash, console, cupholders, and door panels cleaned
  • Interior trim and plastic surfaces wiped down
  • Floor mats cleaned
  • Interior glass cleaned

Outside

  • Exterior hand wash
  • Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned
  • Tire dressing applied
  • Exterior windows and mirrors cleaned
  • Door jamb wipe-down
  • Spray protection for added gloss

Choose how often we come

Every 2 weeks

For vehicles that see heavy daily use: long commutes, kids, pets, or lots of road grime.

Monthly

A good fit for most daily drivers, and what we recommend for ceramic-coated vehicles to help the coating last.

Every 2 months

For vehicles that are garaged or driven less, so they stay clean between longer gaps.

Who can join

Maintenance plans are for approved customers whose vehicles are already in good condition. A first-time or heavily soiled vehicle may need a regular Full Detail first. If a vehicle's condition later goes beyond maintenance-level work, we may pause the plan until it's back in shape.
