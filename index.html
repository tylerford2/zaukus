Our Services

Detailing services designed to protect, refine, and preserve your vehicle.

Zaukus Mobile Detailing provides professional auto detailing, paint correction, and ceramic coating throughout Middle Tennessee, bringing professional equipment to your driveway or office for careful, paint-safe work without a shop visit. Every step, from the first rinse to the final walkthrough, follows the same process we would use in a shop, just parked in your driveway.

Professional Mobile Detailing

More than a clean car. Our exterior wash uses a slick polymer soap that lifts dirt off the paint instead of dragging it across, so washing doesn't leave swirl marks or soapy residue behind. We finish drying with compressed air to clear water out of trim, mirrors, and seams. Inside, we deep-clean every high-touch surface and clean upholstery without leaving greasy, dust-attracting dressings behind. Packages are priced by vehicle size, from coupes to vans and fleet vehicles.

  • Interior Detail
  • Exterior Wash & Protection
  • Full Detail
  • Sized for coupe through van/fleet

Paint Correction

Paint correction permanently removes swirl marks and light marring instead of hiding them under a glaze. We machine polish the clear coat, the paint's top protective layer, until it is smooth and glossy again. Our polishes are low-dust, so trim, plastics, and body lines stay clean instead of caked with white residue.

  • One-Step Enhancement
  • Two-Step Correction
  • Touch-Up Paint

Ceramic Coating

A ceramic coating bonds to your paint and forms a hard, slick layer of protection. It adds gloss, makes water bead and roll off, resists stains from bird droppings, bugs, and road grime, and makes routine washes much easier. We'll help you choose the right coating for your vehicle during a consultation.

Why Coatings Start With a Consultation

A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so the paint has to be fully cleaned and corrected first. That prep is different for every vehicle, so we quote coatings after seeing the paint in person. Select "Book a Consultation" in our online lead form to get started.

Pricing is based on your vehicle's size and the condition of the paint. Custom options and fleet quotes are available through our Fieldd booking platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a detail take?

Professional details typically take 2-4 hours depending on your vehicle's size and condition. We'll provide a time estimate when you book.

Do you use safe products?

Yes. We hand wash with gentle, paint-safe products, including a slick polymer wash that lifts dirt off the paint instead of dragging it across the surface. No harsh chemicals and no automated brushes.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Professional coatings last for years, but how long depends on the coating you choose and how the vehicle is cared for. We cover durability for each option during your consultation, and any rating is a baseline, not a guarantee. Harsh automatic washes or neglected maintenance can wear down even our best coating quickly. We recommend monthly maintenance to help maximize longevity.

Can you remove swirl marks?

Yes. Paint correction removes swirl marks, light marring, and haze by machine polishing the clear coat, and our low-dust polishes keep trim and body lines clean. We don't do deep damage repair, body work, or repainting; anything deep enough to catch a fingernail is beyond what polishing can safely fix.

Do you work in winter?

We work year-round! We take extra precautions in cold weather to ensure product application and drying are optimal.

What areas do you service?

We primarily service La Vergne, TN and surrounding Middle Tennessee areas. Contact us to confirm service availability for your location.
