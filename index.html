Questions

Common questions

How long does a detail take?

Most details take 2 to 5 hours depending on your vehicle's size and condition. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. We bring our own water and electric equipment to every appointment, so all we need is a safe spot to work.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We work year-round, but we may reschedule for rain, storms, freezing temperatures, or other conditions that affect safety or results. If weather moves your appointment, we'll find the next date that works for you.

Can you remove swirl marks?

Yes. Paint correction removes swirl marks, light marring, and haze by machine polishing the clear coat. We don't do body work or repainting; damage deep enough to catch a fingernail is beyond what polishing can safely fix.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Years, but it depends on the coating you choose and how the vehicle is cared for. We cover durability for each option during your free paint check, and any rating is a baseline, not a guarantee.
