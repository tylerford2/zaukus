Mobile Detailing Packages for La Vergne & Middle Tennessee

Professional detailing from Zaukus combines a paint-safe exterior wash with a thorough interior deep clean, delivered to your home or office throughout Middle Tennessee. We bring our own water tank, pressure washer, generator, and air compressor, so every package follows the same process whether we're parked in a downtown garage or a rural driveway. Every package is sized to your vehicle, from coupes to full-size vans and fleets.

A proper detail is about more than a clean car. Our wash starts with a slick polymer soap that loosens grit and carries it off the surface, so the wash itself never becomes the source of new swirl marks, and it rinses clean without soapy film. Compressed air finishes the drying by clearing water out of trim and seams. Inside the cabin, we deep-clean every high-touch surface and clean upholstery without the greasy, dust-attracting residue that store-bought products tend to leave.

Every vehicle is different, so pricing and time scale by size, but all three packages above are available no matter what you drive. Find your vehicle's category below.

Interior Detail includes

  • Full interior vacuum
  • Compressed air blowout of cracks, crevices, vents, and trim gaps
  • Dash, console, cupholders, and door panels cleaned
  • Interior trim and plastic surfaces wiped down
  • Floor mats cleaned
  • Door jamb wipe-down
  • Interior glass cleaned
  • Light deodorizer

Exterior Wash & Protection includes

  • Exterior hand wash
  • Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned
  • Tire dressing applied
  • Exterior windows and mirrors cleaned
  • Door jamb wipe-down
  • Light bug and road grime cleanup
  • Spray protection applied for added gloss

Full Detail includes

  • Every step from Interior Detail above
  • Every step from Exterior Wash & Protection above
  • Completed together in one mobile visit
